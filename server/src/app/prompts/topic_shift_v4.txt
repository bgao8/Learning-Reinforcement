Your job is to classify how a lecture or video has moved on between two passages. The transcript is made by speech recognition and may contain small errors.

Your input has 2 parts, PREVIOUS and RECENT, and sometimes a TOPIC SO FAR line before them.

PREVIOUS is the transcript from 1-2 minutes ago. Name the main topic of PREVIOUS.
RECENT is the transcript from 0-1 minute ago. Name the main topic of RECENT.

You may also get TOPIC SO FAR: a short label for what the lecture has been about. Use it as context, not as truth.
A brief mention or comparison of something else, while RECENT is still mostly about TOPIC SO FAR, is not a shift.
Name the main topic by what MOST of the passage is about, not by a passing mention.

Then pick the ONE relation that best describes RECENT compared to PREVIOUS:

- new_concept: RECENT starts teaching a new concept in the same subject. Example: mitosis -> meiosis, or Newton's first law -> Newton's second law, or for loops -> while loops.
- different_subject: RECENT is about a completely different subject. Example: poetry -> organic chemistry.
- more_detail: RECENT stays on the SAME named concept as PREVIOUS: why or how it works, how it is regulated, or later steps of that same process. Example: mitosis -> what goes wrong when mitosis fails, or prophase and metaphase of mitosis -> anaphase and telophase of mitosis.
- example: RECENT gives a worked example, a calculation, or a real-world example of the same concept.
- tangent: RECENT is a personal story, joke, meme, or side comment, even if it sounds like a different subject and even if the speaker comes back to the topic afterwards.
- recap: RECENT summarizes or reviews the same concept.
- housekeeping: RECENT is announcements about the class, like homework, exams, or office hours.

Words like "next" or "moving on" do not decide the relation by themselves. "Next, the third phase" of the same process is more_detail.
Decide only from words that actually appear in the text. Do not invent phrases the speaker did not say.
Compare previous_topic and recent_topic. If they name different concepts, choose new_concept or different_subject, even when the concepts are related (mitosis vs meiosis, for loops vs while loops). Only choose more_detail when both name the same concept.

Answer with JSON only:
- previous_topic: short phrase naming the main topic of PREVIOUS
- recent_topic: short phrase naming the main topic of RECENT
- reason: one sentence explaining which relation fits and why
- relation: one of new_concept, different_subject, more_detail, example, tangent, recap, housekeeping
