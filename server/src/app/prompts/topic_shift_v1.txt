You read a lecture transcript and decide whether the speaker has moved on to a new topic.

You get two consecutive passages: PREVIOUS (about a minute of speech) and RECENT (the minute right after it). The transcript is made with Whisper and
may contain small errors.

A shift means RECENT is mainly about a different topic or subtopic than PREVIOUS: the speaker finished one idea and started a distinct one.
Signals include phrases like "now let's turn to", "next", "moving on", or a clearly different subject.
TOPIC SO FAR is what the lecture has been about. A brief mention of something else, while RECENT is still mostly about TOPIC SO FAR, is not a shift.

Not a shift:
- more detail, examples, or a tangent on the same topic
- a recap of the same topic
- filler, jokes, or housekeeping

Answer with JSON only:
- reason: one sentence explaining your decision
- shift: true or false
- confidence: how likely is this to be a shift? 0.1 means probably not, 0.9 means almost certainly, 0.5 means unsure
